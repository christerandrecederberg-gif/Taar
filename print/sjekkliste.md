# Tår sjekklister

Rediger punktene her. `## ` starter en ny side (liste), `### ` en seksjon, `- ` et punkt.
Kjør `python3 print/lag-pdf.py` for å lage `Taar-sjekkliste.pdf`.

## WEEKLY OPENING CHECKLIST
Undertittel: Opening, stocking and bar setup
Initialer: Opener initials


### Opening routines
- Turn on the dishwasher
- Turn on the espresso machine
- Vacuum the floors, including under the tables
- Wash the floors
- Check that the tables are clean
- Clean the lamps
- Check that tables, chairs, lamps, and menus are neatly arranged
- Check menus: replace if needed and place them correctly on the tables
- Clean and arrange the outdoor tables and chairs
- Pick up cigarette butts around our area
- Check the toilets: tidy up and restock paper towels and toilet paper

### Stocking & bar setup
- Fill the ice wells
- Fill the ice well for the pre-diluted batches
- Juice limes and lemons, and make the lime/lemon mix
- Restock the drink batches
- Restock the syrups
- Restock glasses and ice in the small freezer at the bar
- Restock ice cubes for the big freezer at the bar
- Restock the fridges at the bar
- Check stock of milk, cream, cucumber, etc.
- Set out and restock the garnish drawer
- Herbs at the bar: Remove dead leaves, water if needed, and swap with herbs upstairs if needed
- Herbs upstairs: water the Thai basil (5 dl per bucket), and the mint if it's drying out
- Check and restock the fridge upstairs if needed (backup light beer and sodas)
- Take out the glass recycling
- Raise the curtains
- Put out the “We're open” signs
- Open the backyard gate

## WEEKLY CLOSING CHECKLIST
Undertittel: Closing, cleaning and security
Initialer: Closer initials


### Cleaning & reset
- Bring in the “We're open” signs
- Put all syrups, cheater bottles, citrus, cherries, etc. in the fridges
- Put the citrus/garnish drawer in the fridge
- Clean the bar, the coffee area and the freezers
- Wipe down all surfaces behind the bar, including the fridges and dishwasher
- Wash the tables with cleaning solution
- Arrange all tables, chairs, lamps, and menus neatly
- Drain the dishwasher
- Clean the espresso machine with cleaning powder (if it was used)
- Clean the portafilter (if it was used): take it apart, soak it in a box with cleaning powder for 20 minutes, then rinse it thoroughly
- Clean the coffee area upstairs (if it was used) (coffee machine, table, and thermos)
- Take out the trash, downstairs and upstairs
- Charge all table lamps
- Set out the freshly charged lamps

### Security check
- Check that all windows at the bar and upstairs are closed
- Lower the curtains
- Check that the two doors upstairs are locked
- Check that the backyard gate is locked
- Check that the two hallway doors are locked (street and backyard)
- Check that the four doors in the bar are locked
